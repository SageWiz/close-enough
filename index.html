Close Enough

A quick quiz about how far your circle of closeness reaches, and how much physical contact you're into. You end up as a dot on a chart.

20 questions, a few minutes. Most of them ask "who can..." and you pick the widest ring you'd be okay with. Everyone inside that ring counts too.

Who's in each ring

People don't always fit neatly, so go with where someone usually sits for you.

Picking a ring includes everyone inside it.

What gets saved

Only if the box is checked. Uncheck it and nothing leaves your device.

  • Your 20 answers and the scores they add up to
  • How long the quiz took you, overall and per question
  • Whether you were on a phone or a computer
  • The date and time you finished
  • Which shared link you came from, if it had a tag (like Instagram)
  • Whether this is your first time finishing on this device

What doesn't

The quiz never asks for your name, email or location, and it doesn't use cookies or tie your answers to you. Your browser keeps a count of how many times you've finished, only so retakes can be marked. That count stays on your device. Like any website, the database host keeps short-lived server logs.

Where it goes

Results feed a public stats page. Individual answers can show up there, but with nothing that says who they came from.
